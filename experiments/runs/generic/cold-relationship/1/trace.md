# Deliberation trace: cold-relationship

> The CRM should alert us when our relationship with a country goes cold.

Policy `gated` · models: proposer gpt-4o-mini, critic gpt-4o-mini, summarizer gpt-4o-mini · ended **converged** after 3 rounds · 10 model calls (3 repairs)

## Round 1

### Proposer · confidence 75

_This release will implement an alert system that notifies users when engagement levels with a country drop below a defined threshold, aimed at regional coordinators and project managers. The feature will help maintain proactive communication and relationship management with government counterparts._

**Core commitments**
- V1: Enable users to receive alerts when engagement levels with any country drop below a specified threshold.

**In scope**
- S1: Define 'cold' engagement as any country that has had no contact recorded in the system for a period of 6 months.
- S2: Implement a notification system that sends alerts to regional coordinators and project managers when a relationship with a country is classified as 'cold'.
- S3: Allow users to configure the alert notification settings within their user profiles.
- S4: Provide a dashboard widget that displays countries whose relationships are currently classified as 'cold'.

**Out of scope**
- X1: This release will not include integrations with external notification systems (e.g., email alerts, SMS alerts).
- X2: This release will not allow users to change the definition of 'cold' to a time frame other than 6 months.

**Assumptions**
- A1: Users have access to the CRM system where relationships and engagements are logged, which the alert feature depends on.
- A2: User roles are already established within the CRM, allowing for differentiated access to features such as alerts, which this proposal relies on.
- A3: The organization has defined engagement activities that can be tracked in order to determine when a relationship is classified as 'cold'.

**Definitions**
- D1: 'Cold' engagement means having no documented contact recorded between the organization and a country within the last 6 months.
- D2: 'Alert notification' means a message generated by the CRM system that informs users of a change in a relationship status.
- D3: 'Users' refers to regional coordinators and project managers who have the authority to manage relationships with government counterparts.

**Success criteria**
- K1: At least 80% of users receive the alert notification within 24 hours of the engagement status being classified as 'cold', measured through user feedback and system logs.

Biggest worry: The criteria for engagement tracking and alerts may not align with the organization's current logging practices.

### Critic · confidence 30

An alert regarding a country designated as 'cold' reached a project manager who was unaware of prior engagements due to access restrictions; this led to unnecessary outreach attempts and strained relationships, harming the organization's credibility.

Questions the request leaves open:
- Which user roles can see alerts related to cold relationships?
- What happens if a project manager changes or leaves their role?
- What criteria determine who gets notified when a relationship is classified as 'cold'?
- What precautions are in place to ensure engagement history is accurate to avoid false alerts?
- How long will 'cold' engagement records be retained, and what happens to them after that period?
- Are there parameters to distinguish between cold relationships and relationships with minimal contact?

## Final proposal

_This release will implement an alert system that notifies users when engagement levels with a country drop below a defined threshold, with added measures for data accuracy to enhance compliance. It aims to help maintain proactive communication and relationship management with government counterparts, with clarified responsibilities and criteria for alert notifications._

**Core commitments**
- V1: Enable users to receive alerts when engagement levels with any country drop below a specified threshold.

**In scope**
- S1: Define 'cold' engagement as any country that has had no formal documented contact recorded in the system for a period of 6 months, while including informal engagements that are logged as exceptions. Implement a verification process that ensures the engagement history is accurate, which includes a regular audit of logged engagements every 3 months.
- S2: Implement a notification system that sends alerts only to regional coordinators and project managers who are actively assigned to manage the country in the CRM, ensuring that notifications are limited to authorized personnel.
- S3: Allow users to configure the alert notification settings within their user profiles.
- S4: Provide a dashboard widget that displays countries whose relationships are currently classified as 'cold'.

**Out of scope**
- X1: This release will not include integrations with external notification systems (e.g., email alerts, SMS alerts).
- X2: This release will not allow users to change the definition of 'cold' to a time frame other than 6 months.

**Assumptions**
- A1: Users have access to the CRM system where relationships and engagements are logged, which the alert feature depends on.
- A2: User roles are already established within the CRM, allowing for differentiated access to features such as alerts, which this proposal relies on.
- A3: The organization has defined engagement activities that can be tracked in order to determine when a relationship is classified as 'cold'.

**Definitions**
- D1: 'Cold' engagement means having no documented contact recorded between the organization and a country within the last 6 months.
- D2: 'Alert notification' means a message generated by the CRM system that informs users of a change in a relationship status.
- D3: 'Users' refers to regional coordinators and project managers who have the authority to manage relationships with government counterparts.

**Success criteria**
- K1: At least 80% of users receive the alert notification within 24 hours of the engagement status being classified as 'cold', measured through user feedback and system logs.
